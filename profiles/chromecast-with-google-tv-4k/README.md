# Chromecast with Google TV (4K & HD)

- **Manufacturer:** Google
- **Board / SoC:** Amlogic S905X3
- **OS Version:** Android TV 12 (Google TV interface)

## Key Benefits
- Replaces heavy stock Google TV LauncherX (`com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx`) which consumes 220+ MB RAM for video trailer caching.
- Frees **~180 MB of RAM** for streaming apps.
- Pairs with Projectivy Launcher for instant 0.0x home screen navigation while maintaining "Watch Next" streaming rows.
